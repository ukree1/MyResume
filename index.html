about me

Who I am, in short

I'm an IT student at Dominican College of Tarlac with a growing passion for networking, troubleshooting, and system administration. I enjoy the moment a broken connection gets diagnosed and fixed — there's something satisfying about tracing a problem back to its root cause, whether it's a misconfigured VLAN or a flaky cable.

Alongside my networking studies, I build practical software — from record-keeping systems to support ticketing tools — because I believe good IT support means understanding both the hardware layer and the applications running on top of it.

My goal is straightforward: start in IT Support, grow into a Network Technician, and eventually become a Network Engineer who designs and maintains the infrastructure organizations depend on.
